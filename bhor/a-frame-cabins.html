A Frame Cabins

Modern alpine cabins with glass facades, private plunge pools, and scenic views. Perfect for longer stays and a complete urban detox.

Triangular alpine designed cabins are modern yet cozy. These cabins face east and adorn a large glass façade to provide the mesmerizing views of the backwaters in winters and scintillating view mountains in summers. Each room comes with private plunge pool and attached washrooms and small deck area to offer complete luxury. Enjoy your longer stays here and get a full urban detox!
