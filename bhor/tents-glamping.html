Tents and Glamping

Lakeside glamping and tents with stunning views, 24-hour café, and rustic amenities. Perfect for groups seeking a unique camping experience.

Experience the joys of camping with friends, family and colleagues at our pristine glamping site by the lake. All our tents and glamps offer some of the most exhilarating views, an amazing 24 hrs café with super amenities within a rustic and natural setting. A great place to relax and enjoy the evening!

Waterboard Glamps: Our most famous and hottest selling units. An experience of a lifetime at a pocket friendly price! Just a foot above the water level (in season) & the expansive lake ahead there is nothing quite like this around Pune and Mumbai. Enjoy the sunrise and the silver waters on a moon night and the comfy beds to snooze.
