Our Journey

Nestled amidst nature, Waterrock’s journey began with a vision to blend our busy lives with nature. Our story is one of passion, innovation, and a deep respect for the environment.

Waterrock was founded by couple with passion to travel and discover offbeat stays. Waterrock is your escape into nature’s lap where comfort meets adventure and every meal feels like home.Tucked away from the noise of the city, our offbeat resort is designed for those who crave something different — a place where you can wake up to birdsong, sip chai by the lake, and end the day under a sky full of stars. We believe in simple joys: the smell of fresh earth after rain, the warmth of a cozy room, and the magic of a well-cooked meal. Our menu brings soul-satisfying dishes, made with love and fresh ingredients.Whether you're here to unwind, explore, or just breathe a little deeper, we’re here to make you feel at home — naturally.Over the years, Waterrock has grown into a vibrant community, welcoming 4000+ guests from around the world to experience the harmony of nature and refined living. Our journey continues, guided by passion, innovation, and respect for the earth.
At Waterrock, we believe in the power of connection—between people and nature. Our mission is to create unforgettable experiences that inspire guests to cherish the natural world. We invite you to join us on this journey, where every moment is an opportunity to discover the beauty of life in harmony with nature.
